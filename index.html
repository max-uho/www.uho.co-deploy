UHO is an architecture studio. It ranges from theory to construction in scope and from architectural to urban in scale. Projects include housing, schools and public space.

UHO has been involved in teaching since its inception (AA, ÉNSAPM, ÉSA, KIT Karlsruhe, Politecnico di Torino…) and its intuitions are synthesised in a book: The Use of Space.
